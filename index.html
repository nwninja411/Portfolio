A Little About Me

Hello and welcome to my portfolio! My name is Noah Walker, and I graduated from the University of Advancing Technology with a Bachelor's degree in Digital Video. I'm a video editor and post-production artist with a passion for visual storytelling and bringing creative ideas to life. I have over four years of experience working with Adobe Creative Cloud, along with hands-on experience editing promotional, educational, and event videos.

Growing up, I always loved going to the movie theater with family and friends. I was fascinated by how a team of individuals, each bringing their own skills and creative perspectives, could come together to tell a compelling story. That fascination inspired me to pursue a career in video production, with a particular interest in post-production.

My primary focus is video editing, but I also have experience in sound design, motion graphics, and visual effects compositing. I'm proficient in Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Audition, with additional experience in Photoshop, Lightroom, Maya, and Nuke.
